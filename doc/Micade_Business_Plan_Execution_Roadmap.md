# Micade Business Plan

## Section 4: Execution Roadmap

Status: Working draft
Phase: Phase 1 - Build the Company
Owner: Enterprise Architecture Agent
Supporting agent: Business Architecture Agent

## 4.1 Section Purpose

This section defines Micade's practical execution path for the first operating period.

The roadmap should help Micade move from documentation into controlled business action without skipping validation, delivery quality, or governance.

## 4.2 Approved Starting Point

- Micade is an integrated digital growth company.
- First priority audience: small businesses.
- First launch segment: local service businesses.
- Starting model: service-led growth.
- First approved packages: Digital Presence Starter, Growth System Setup, AI and Automation Assist.
- Recommended pricing direction: hybrid pricing.
- Recommended recurring revenue direction: optional monthly support.

## 4.3 First 90-Day Roadmap

Month 1: Foundation and Validation

Focus:

- Finalize Brand Guidelines, Company Blueprint, and Business Plan.
- Choose first local service niches.
- Create discovery questionnaire.
- Create digital growth audit checklist.
- Create proposal template.
- Build simple lead tracker.
- Identify first 30 outreach targets.
- Run early customer interviews and discovery calls.

Primary outcome:

Micade has a clear offer, target customer, outreach list, and validation process.

Month 2: Outreach and First Projects

Focus:

- Begin direct outreach, referrals, and educational content.
- Test Digital Growth Audit as an entry offer.
- Refine packages based on customer feedback.
- Close first service projects where possible.
- Document objections, questions, and common customer problems.
- Create first project delivery checklists.

Primary outcome:

Micade validates early demand and starts turning real customer conversations into service and content improvements.

Month 3: Delivery and Repeatability

Focus:

- Deliver first projects with strong documentation.
- Capture lessons learned from each project.
- Improve package scopes and support boundaries.
- Create reusable templates from delivery.
- Define monthly support offer.
- Prepare first case study format.
- Decide next priority: education asset or automation kit.

Primary outcome:

Micade begins building repeatable delivery assets and a clearer path to recurring revenue.

## 4.4 First 90-Day Targets

Proposed targets:

- Complete 3 core Phase 1 documents: Brand Guidelines, Company Blueprint, and Business Plan.
- Choose 2 or 3 local service niches.
- Build 1 discovery questionnaire.
- Build 1 digital growth audit checklist.
- Build 1 proposal template.
- Build 1 lead tracker.
- Identify 30 outreach targets.
- Complete 10 customer interviews or discovery conversations.
- Send 5 qualified proposals.
- Complete or begin 1 to 3 starter projects.
- Create 1 reusable delivery checklist.
- Create 1 case study template.

These are proposed planning targets, not guaranteed business outcomes.

## 4.5 Execution Priorities

Priority 1: Finish the Business Foundation

Micade should complete the Business Plan before moving into full website specification or public launch work.

Priority 2: Validate the Customer Segment

Micade should speak with local service business owners before locking exact pricing, package scope, and niche focus.

Priority 3: Package the Service Offers

Each starter package should have clear scope, deliverables, exclusions, timeline, and support options.

Priority 4: Build Delivery Assets

Micade should create reusable checklists, questionnaires, proposals, and client communication templates.

Priority 5: Start Controlled Outreach

Outreach should test the offer and customer problem fit without overpromising outcomes.

## 4.6 Key Performance Indicators

Proposed early KPIs:

- Outreach targets identified
- Discovery conversations completed
- Customer problems documented
- Proposals sent
- Proposal acceptance rate
- Starter projects started
- Starter projects completed
- Client satisfaction
- Reusable templates created
- Monthly support interest
- Repeatable automation opportunities identified
- Case studies created

## 4.7 Risk Management

Key execution risks:

- Moving to website design before business decisions are clear
- Choosing too many customer niches
- Pricing before understanding customer willingness to pay
- Offering unclear package scope
- Failing to document delivery lessons
- Taking projects that do not match Micade's direction
- Selling AI or automation without proper boundaries

Risk controls:

- Keep the Phase 1 document sequence intact.
- Limit first outreach to 2 or 3 niches.
- Use discovery calls before final pricing.
- Define package inclusions and exclusions.
- Capture lessons learned from every client conversation and project.
- Review all public claims against brand governance.

## 4.9 Decisions Captured

- Micade's first execution period should focus on foundation, validation, outreach, and repeatable delivery.
- The first 90 days should prioritize practical validation over aggressive scaling.
- Business Plan completion should come before full website technical specification.
- Exact pricing should wait until initial validation unless the project owner approves a starting range.
- Outreach and delivery must create reusable business assets.

## 4.10 Why These Decisions Matter

This roadmap gives Micade a disciplined way to start.

It connects documentation, customer validation, service packaging, outreach, delivery, and learning into one operating sequence.
