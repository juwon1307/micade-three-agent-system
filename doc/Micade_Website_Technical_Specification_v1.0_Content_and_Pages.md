# Micade Website Technical Specification v1.0

## Section 2: Content and Pages

Status: Working draft
Phase: Phase 1 - Build the Company
Owner: Technical Specification Agent
Supporting agents: Brand Strategy Agent, Business Architecture Agent

## 2.1 Content Strategy

The launch website should make Micade easy to understand and easy to contact for small businesses, especially local service businesses. Content should lead with practical business outcomes: a clearer digital presence, a repeatable growth system, and useful automation.

The content hierarchy is:

1. Explain what Micade does.
2. Show who Micade helps.
3. Present the approved service packages.
4. Establish credibility through process, proof, and useful resources.
5. Move qualified visitors toward a discovery conversation or audit request.

Content should use the approved voice: balanced, professional, practical, and clear. It should avoid inflated claims, unexplained technical language, and promises of guaranteed growth.

## 2.2 Launch Navigation

Proposed primary navigation:

- Home
- About
- Services
- Resources
- Contact

The Services page links to the three initial package pages:

- Digital Presence Starter
- Growth System Setup
- AI and Automation Assist

## 2.3 Page Requirements

### Homepage

Establish Micade's position and route visitors to the right next action. It must state that Micade is an integrated digital growth company, identify small businesses and local service businesses as the first audience, summarize the three initial packages, explain the engagement process, and provide Start a conversation and Explore services actions.

### About

Explain Micade's story, purpose, integrated digital growth company positioning, and practical approach to responsible technology. Link visitors to services or contact.

### Services

Help visitors identify the right starting point through a package comparison covering business problem, outcome, scope, and next step. Link to each package page and provide an audit or conversation action.

### Service Package Pages

Each package page must describe the problem addressed, best-fit customer, outcomes, deliverables, delivery stages, customer inputs, scope boundaries, pricing position where approved, FAQs, and the Start a conversation action.

### Resources and Blog

Resources should support trust, discovery, and reusable SEO/GEO content. Each item needs a title, summary, content type, audience, problem, author, publication/update dates, related service, structured headings, practical next steps, and a relevant CTA.

### Contact

The inquiry form should capture name, email, business name, website or social link, business type, main growth challenge, and preferred next step. It must include privacy notice, validation errors, a success state, and an alternative contact path.

## 2.4 CTA Hierarchy

Primary CTA across the launch site: Start a conversation.

Contextual actions:

- Services: Explore the right starting package.
- Package pages: Request an audit or start a conversation.
- Resources: Apply this to my business.
- Contact: Send inquiry.

CTA labels should describe the action and avoid pressure-based language. Destinations and conversion events must be defined before implementation.

## 2.5 SEO and GEO Requirements

Every indexable page should define a unique title tag, meta description, H1, heading structure, canonical URL, social preview data, descriptive alt text, and internal links. Relevant pages should use accurate Organization, Service, Article, BreadcrumbList, and FAQPage structured data when visible content supports it.

GEO readiness should come from clear definitions, direct answers, consistent company facts, useful examples, and crawlable page content. Unsupported claims must not be generated or published.

## 2.6 Content Operations

Every page should have an owner, status, source notes, review date, and approval state. Claims about results, client work, pricing, partnerships, or capabilities require explicit approval before publication.

Initial dependencies are the approved company description, final package scope and pricing language, team information if used, approved proof points, contact and privacy details, and the first resource topics with publishing owners.

## 2.7 Decisions Captured

- Launch content prioritizes small businesses and local service businesses.
- Primary navigation focuses on Home, About, Services, Resources, and Contact.
- Each approved service package has a dedicated detail page.
- The primary CTA is Start a conversation, supported by contextual audit and service actions.
- SEO and GEO are part of the content model.
- Public claims require evidence and approval.
