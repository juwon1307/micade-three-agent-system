# Micade Website Content Briefs v1.0

Status: Working draft
Audience: Small businesses, starting with local service businesses
Voice: Balanced, professional, practical, and clear
Working tagline: Learn. Build. Grow.

## Content Rules

- Explain Micade as an integrated digital growth company.
- Lead with practical business outcomes.
- Use evidence-based claims only.
- Mark pricing, proof points, CTA destinations, and provider details as proposed until approved.
- Keep content accessible, scannable, and useful on mobile.

## Homepage

Job: Explain Micade quickly and guide a qualified visitor toward the next action.

Required sections: positioning and tagline, audience and problems served, three service summaries, engagement process, approved proof, direct-answer FAQ, Start a conversation CTA, and Explore services CTA.

Acceptance: a first-time visitor can explain what Micade does, identify relevance, understand the starting services, and reach the inquiry path.

## About Page

Job: Explain why Micade exists and how its integrated growth approach works.

Required sections: purpose and story, integrated digital growth explanation, Learn. Build. Grow. idea, responsible technology principles, current service-led starting point, and future direction clearly marked as future.

Acceptance: the page builds trust without unsupported experience, scale, or outcome claims.

## Services Page

Job: Help visitors select an appropriate starting package.

Required sections: service model, comparison by problem/outcome/scope/best fit, included and custom scope, engagement process, customer inputs, and FAQs.

Acceptance: visitors can compare Digital Presence Starter, Growth System Setup, and AI and Automation Assist and reach each detail page.

## Service Package Pages

Each package page must cover the problem, best-fit customer, outcomes, deliverables, delivery stages, customer inputs, scope boundaries, proposed pricing position, FAQs, and next step.

Digital Presence Starter: for local service businesses with an unclear, outdated, or inconsistent digital presence.

Growth System Setup: for local service businesses that need a more repeatable marketing and lead process.

AI and Automation Assist: for small businesses with repetitive work or clear opportunities for practical automation, with human oversight and privacy boundaries made explicit.

Acceptance: a suitable business can determine fit and understand what engagement would require without encountering guaranteed-result claims.

## Contact Page

Required fields: name, email, business name, business type, main challenge, preferred next step, and optional website or social link.

Required states: initial, validation error, submission error, success, and fallback contact path.

Acceptance: visitors can submit a valid inquiry, understand what happens next, and recover from errors without losing entered information.

## Shared SEO and GEO Requirements

Each page needs a unique title, meta description, H1, heading outline, canonical URL, social preview data, descriptive image alt text, internal links, and approved structured data where applicable. Use direct answers, consistent Micade facts, clear service definitions, and crawlable content.

## Review Gate

Before implementation, approve the final CTA, service scope, pricing language, proof points, About story, Contact privacy language, and launch page list.

## Recommended Next Implementation Step

Approve these briefs, then create the website project and design-system foundation.
