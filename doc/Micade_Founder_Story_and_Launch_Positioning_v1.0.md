# Micade Founder Story and Launch Positioning v1.0

Status: Working source of truth
Last updated: 21 September 2026

## Purpose

This document records the human story and first market focus behind Micade. Public copy should use these facts instead of generic technology-company language.

## Verified Founder Story

- The founder studied Computer Science for both OND and HND at Lagos State Polytechnic, Ikorodu.
- The OND programme began in the 2007/2008 academic session and ended in 2010/2011.
- The HND programme began in the 2017/2018 academic session and ended in 2020.
- After the HND, the founder pursued frontend development as a professional skill.
- A former coursemate who became a programmer and data analyst encouraged the founder's journey.
- Learning programming was difficult at first. Financial pressure and difficulty understanding some concepts made the journey slower, but the founder continued learning gradually.
- In 2022, after completing a frontend development programme, the founder joined Semicolon Africa as an intern and worked with a team on a project.
- During this period, the founder also experienced an accident. After recovering, the founder restarted from the fundamentals instead of abandoning programming.
- In 2024, the founder began teaching HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, and JavaScript at Uptech Computer Training Academy.
- The founder has taught approximately 100 students as of September 2026.
- One former student was encouraged and supported through calls and Google Meet sessions as she became an instructor. She now teaches children outside Nigeria remotely through a school.
- The founder later added digital marketing to frontend development and teaching experience.

## Human Meaning

Micade is shaped by a founder who knows what it feels like to struggle with technology, start again, learn step by step, and explain difficult ideas patiently to someone else.

The combination behind Micade is:

1. Frontend development to build the digital experience.
2. Digital marketing to help the right people discover it.
3. Teaching to make the process understandable to the business owner.

## Approved First Launch Audience

Micade's first focused audience is:

> Fashion designers in Lagos who depend heavily on Instagram and WhatsApp and need a more professional digital home for their work.

The wider long-term audience may still include other small businesses, learners, and organizations, but they should not compete with this launch message on the main website.

## Observed Problem Hypothesis

The current hypothesis is that many independent fashion designers in Lagos:

- Depend mainly on Instagram and WhatsApp.
- Struggle to present their clothing and collections professionally.
- Find it difficult to attract new customers consistently.
- Do not have an owned digital destination that organizes their story, work, and customer next steps.

These are working assumptions, not yet verified customer research. Micade should interview at least 10 Lagos fashion designers before treating the language, priorities, packages, or pricing as final.

## Launch Positioning

### Clear description

Micade helps fashion designers in Lagos build a professional digital presence they can own, understand, and grow.

### Positioning statement

For fashion designers in Lagos who rely heavily on Instagram and WhatsApp, Micade combines frontend development, digital marketing, and patient guidance to create a professional digital home for their brand and a clearer path for customer discovery.

### Human promise

We will not leave you confused by the technology. We will explain what we are building, why it matters, and how it supports your fashion business.

## Evidence Rules

- Say "approximately 100 students taught" or "about 100 students taught," not "100+" unless the count is verified.
- Do not claim that Micade has already increased fashion-business sales, leads, or revenue.
- Do not present the audience problems as proven facts until customer interviews confirm them.
- Do not publish the name of a friend, student, or other private person without their permission.
- Describe future AI, SaaS, community, events, and certification plans as future direction, not current offers.

## Next Validation Questions

Ask Lagos fashion designers:

1. How do customers currently discover and contact you?
2. What becomes difficult when orders and enquiries happen through Instagram or WhatsApp?
3. How do you currently show collections, prices, sizing, and availability?
4. What would make you trust someone to build a website or digital system for your brand?
5. What result would make the investment worthwhile?
6. What have you already tried, and why did it not work?
